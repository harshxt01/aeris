# Security policy

## Supported versions

Only the latest version on the `main` branch, which is what the live site runs, gets security fixes.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **don't** open a public issue for security problems.

Instead, use GitHub's private reporting: go to the **Security** tab, choose **Report a vulnerability**, and fill in the form. You can also email **harshitchambial@gmail.com**.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and what impact it could have. You'll get a reply within 7 days.

## What Aeris stores

Aeris has no accounts and no server of its own. It saves your last city, temperature unit, sound preference and comparison city in your browser's local storage, plus saved forecasts for offline use. Your location is only used when you press the location button, and is sent only to the weather and place-name services.
