# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Aeris to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity. Beginners are especially welcome.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent
- Give feedback on the work, not the person
- Accept feedback gracefully and learn from mistakes
- Respect different ideas and ways of working

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Any behaviour that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove comments, commits or issues that break this code, and may block anyone who repeatedly does.

To report a problem, email **harshitchambial@gmail.com**. Reports are kept confidential.
